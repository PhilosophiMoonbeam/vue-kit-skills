## Decided
Keep the original path-based lettering in assets/branding/vue-kit-hero.svg. It makes the Vue Kit identity render consistently without an external font. The earlier branding learning accidentally placed this positive design decision under "Tried and ruled out" alongside a rejected raster workflow. The artwork follows the intended design; that section placement is not a prohibition on vector lettering.

## Tried and ruled out
Remote font downloads and a raster wrapper are unnecessary for this self-contained SVG.

## Watch out
Trail check may report the banner as reintroducing geometric wordmark use because it reads the earlier learning's section label literally. Treat that result as a note-classification false positive and preserve the path-based identity. This clarification supersedes that inferred prohibition; it does not change the design.
